# Something Rotten
For the something rotten set, we wanted to re-use the 6' continental parallels that we constructed for Newsies, and incorporate an arched bridge element.
{{< figure src="/images/setdesign/sr-front-color.jpeg" title="Our Something Rotten Set" caption="Now with 400% more painting to do!" width="50%" >}}

Our final set consisted of two additional trapezoidal platforms, and arched bridge, an arched doorway that lead to a set of runaway stairs.

{{< figure src="/images/setdesign/sr-rear-color.jpeg" title="Our Something Rotten Set" caption="Get Jacked!" width="50%" >}}

It also required the construction of 6x 10'6" jacks to support the Broadway flats from floor anchoring.
